## Limitations that must survive the final report

- BC, DAgger and masked PPO were implemented and executed. DQN, discrete SAC, offline CQL/IQL, recurrent policies, attention/entity encoders and MuZero were considered in the plan; they were not all implemented or trained. An extensible environment is not an implementation of every possible learning algorithm.
- Public feature parsing/hash bags, simulator fidelity and some unknown/unsupported mechanics remain limitations. Native search teacher/reference policies are privileged; scripted OOC supervision is not expert strategic search.
- Old native-auto OOC/lookahead evidence is quarantined. Retained legacy full-control combat is a qualified transfer, with later corrected-backend checks; faults and unsupported undefined behavior remain separate from ordinary game deaths.
- The broad hash-final partition was not wholly untouched: historical learned seed-3 integration traces are disclosed in [the protocol audit](nonllm_protocol_audit.md). Seed 3 is outside the designated quantitative final cohorts and excluded from future final inference. Future learned fixtures must be verified train/dev worlds.
- Development OOC horizon 1024 and Act-3 horizon 4096 differ from the planned final horizon 2048. Report censoring and exact act scope; attribute neither differences nor training-round aggregate wins automatically to generalization.
- This first window has demonstrated usable learning and strong selected Act-1 development performance, not full-game mastery. Three-act development has not established victories for the provisional selected policy. The final achieved scope and uncertainty remain to be filled from frozen evidence.
