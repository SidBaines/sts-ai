# Local long-curriculum dashboard

Start from this study worktree; the service binds only 127.0.0.1. It needs no package installation or third-party assets.

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/long_rl_dashboard.py --port 8786
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:8786. Use `--port 0` for an automatically assigned temporary test port, `--campaign PATH` for an explicitly selected campaign, or `--refresh-seconds 10` for a slower telemetry refresh (minimum five seconds). Another process owning the requested port produces a bind failure; choose a free port without stopping unrelated work. Ctrl-C closes only this dashboard. It has no training, pause, deletion or checkpoint-loading controls.

The service exposes only `/api/telemetry`, the filtered `/api/episodes` endpoint, `/`, `/index.html`, `/app.js`, and `/style.css`. The API is normalized telemetry, not raw file access. Training metrics must be campaign-owned `metrics/*.metrics.json` or `checkpoints/*.metrics.json`; symlinks escaping those directories, final-named files, other paths and checkpoint bytes are rejected. The journal/progress/config are fixed document names; external checkpoint identities are displayed without opening the checkpoint. The dashboard never evaluates policies or reads embargoed final cohorts.

Journal/config/progress refresh at most once every eight seconds. Individual metric files are cached by mtime/size plus relevant journal status/evidence, reduced once on change, and retained as compact rows. JSON input has a 64 MiB per-file bound. Static assets use no CDN. The browser keeps compact telemetry plus a bounded filtered episode sample, draws bounded SVG series, and exposes recent records without loading action trajectories or checkpoint bytes. Missing metrics stay unavailable. Auto-refresh can be disabled; manual refresh does not override the server's read rate limit. Poll failures preserve the last good display; heartbeat age and read errors flag stale or missing data.

The active budget is the authoritative persisted active-time ledger, never extrapolated while paused or offline; wall elapsed includes those gaps. Headline transitions/optimizer steps sum actor evidence on journal-committed complete training jobs. Logged metric history may include partial, interrupted or not-yet-committed job outputs; these are separate from committed totals. Job throughput divides valid transitions by the metric's measured job elapsed time; rollout throughput divides by summed collector elapsed time. Policy/value losses, KL, entropy, clip fraction and ratio are arithmetic means of the logged round update summaries per job. Returns are actual episode rewards from non-faulted episodes, including truncated episodes; quantiles interpolate sorted returns. Configured gradient clipping is not a measured gradient norm and never fills that field.

Training family strata preserve start source, starting act, goal act and horizon from the journal's TaskID-bound `family_strata`. Fresh Act 1 rehearsal is not pooled into Act 2/3 success. Native and learner saved-start success is conditional continuation performance; fresh full-start target success is a distinct rate. Requested denominators retain faults/truncations/unstarted tasks. Families accumulate repeated TRAIN sampling and are not independent benchmark worlds. Coverage diagnostics compare requested TaskID and observed episode/fault multisets; incomplete cohorts and collector-budget cutoffs are visible.

DEV charts show every recorded matched-cohort candidate, including rollback candidates as open markers, alongside fixed 41/48, 8/48 and 0/48 full-start baselines. Selected per-goal leaders are distinct from latest candidates and next training parents. An Act 2 selected model's paired Act 1 retention comes from that model's candidate event, not the `dev_act1` initial leader. Reused 48-world DEV gains are descriptive selection evidence, not significance, mastery or final performance. Current may roll back while last checkpoint remains the latest candidate; published hashes are journal metadata, not a live hash verification. Pruning counts come from the controller deletion ledger.

Edit `scripts/long_rl_dashboard.py` for normalization and `web/long_rl_dashboard/{index.html,app.js,style.css}` for presentation. These new files sit outside the frozen learner/controller source set. Do not modify frozen scientific sources or training outputs to add a dashboard metric during an active run. Add new logged telemetry only as a separately reviewed future source condition.

Run focused checks:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m unittest tests.unit.test_long_rl_dashboard
```

These include finite/unavailable metrics, matched TaskID coverage, family separation, pause-ledger semantics, curated paths/symlink rejection, cache invalidation after journal completion and a localhost HTTP smoke on an ephemeral port.



## Plot controls and available metrics

The training explorer uses the actual controller training round, not the interleaved training/evaluation job index. Choose independent left and right metrics; each axis retains its own units. Available job diagnostics include policy/value loss, approximate KL, entropy, clip fraction, accepted optimizer steps, rejected minibatches, ratio mean, and measured rollout/update timing and throughput. Missing gradient norm and rejected KL stay unavailable. Episode measurements include undiscounted return, absolute ending floor, net HP cost, decision count and requested outcome rates.

Use the numeric From/To range, preset history, overview brush, wheel zoom and drag pan. Follow newest advances the view with telemetry; disable it to inspect an earlier period. The rolling choices are raw, 3, 5, 10, 20 or 50 logged round summaries. Smoothing is an unweighted trailing mean of available summaries in the selected view; it does not weight rounds by episode count. Missing current values remain gaps. Hover shows original units, the round and sample/contributor information. Faint raw lines can remain visible alongside smoothing.

Episode controls choose TRAIN, curriculum DEV or full-run observer DEV, plus start source, starting act, goal act, starting-floor bounds, horizon, component, session, outcome and evaluated snapshot role. These filters affect episode means, histograms, scatter plots and sample records. Global PPO losses/timing retain all jobs in the selected round range and explicitly keep their global labels. Outcome filtering makes rates conditional on that outcome-selected sample, so use All outcomes to inspect target success among the selected requested task family. Include cutoffs adds legitimate unfinished episode measurements to numeric plots; faults and unknown measurements remain unavailable.

Histograms expose return, absolute ending floor, signed net HP cost and decision count with selectable bin counts. The family comparison retains faults, cutoffs and unstarted records in loaded requested outcome counts. Scatter plots let you choose numeric axes and color by source, acts, outcome, component or session. The downloadable CSV contains the loaded filtered records, not every episode in the study. The API also exposes signed `floor_progress = ending_floor - starting_floor` when exact start metadata exists; this counts simulator floor transitions from the requested start (including a fresh Neow start at floor 0), not a battle count.

Episode loading is lazy and paginated. Pages carry a stable dataset identity; if the underlying window changes during a multi-page load, the browser discards mixed pages and shows a retry message. JSON responses negotiate gzip; raw JSON remains available to clients that do not request it. The API defaults to the latest 400 matching jobs and permits at most 1,000, with page sizes of 2,000 by default and 20,000 at most; the browser requests up to 1,000 jobs and loads at most 50,000 filtered rows. A wider history can therefore remain a bounded sample. Read the cohort line and coverage counts for matched versus loaded rows, omitted jobs, missing metrics and unjoined metadata before comparing histograms. These are selected episode records, with reused worlds counted again; neither the loaded row count nor multiple DEV measurements establish independent sample sizes.

## Full three-act observer view

The prominent full-run panel reads exactly the sibling `data/long_rl_v1/full_run_eval/telemetry.json` written by the independent observer. It never opens observer checkpoint copies or invokes an evaluation. The full-run episode explorer also accepts exactly the owned initial `anchor_dev_act3.metrics.json`, validated against the original checkpoint identity, fixed DEV TaskIDs, saved metric SHA and condition; this specific exception exposes the initial reference distribution without opening other study files. The observer is monitoring only: its fresh full-start `dev_act3` panel does not change learner ranking, selection, rewards or the final embargo.

INITIAL is the immutable pre-update 2026-10-12 reference: initial SHA `57f1e9613a69e31230b591ef0ff2cca27e23ddc81c3a24366cee173bc9909e70`, 0/48 whole-game wins, 48 defeats, no faults/cutoffs/unstarted tasks, mean floor 28.6042. The initial line is dashed and remains a fixed comparison, not an observed training curve. Selected-best and latest-candidate evaluated snapshots are distinct roles. If both roles share a SHA at one evaluation, one row can carry both roles; the same result is not counted as independent evidence.

A missing observer result is **NOT YET EVALUATED**, never 0/48. Every row carries its exact checkpoint SHA, roles, captured controller round/time, evaluation UTC, requested/outcome counts, completion status, cohort identity check and mean floor. The most recent evaluation may describe older weights than the live role: the dashboard compares SHA identities and says when a newer live role remains unevaluated. Ages refer to evaluation time, not the training checkpoint timestamp. Only complete panels appear in the trend series; incomplete/mismatched panels remain visible in the table.

Observer telemetry is cached by mtime/size under the same refresh bound. A symlink escaping the owned observer directory is rejected. Records require an exact 64-digit checkpoint SHA, recognized role and monitoring-only provenance. Requested TaskID multisets are checked against the campaign's fixed `dev_act3` cohort; 48 unique requested IDs and accounted outcomes are required. Checkpoint bytes and unrestricted metrics paths are never served. The original curriculum DEV tiles remain separately labeled by target; the Act 3 initial leader is explicitly an INITIAL reference.

## Interpreting rollout distributions

Returns, ending floor and HP cost describe the selected episode sample, not a fixed benchmark when the source mixture changes. Keep start source, start act, goal act, component, session and stage visible when comparing periods. Native continuations begin at the first public battle in Act 2/3 (floors 18/35); learner continuations enter those acts at floors 17/34 and may begin in a menu. Fresh scratch episodes begin at Act 1 Neow. A later starting floor therefore raises absolute ending floor without establishing improvement. Return also depends on the target act and distance remaining. TRAIN worlds and saved frontiers can be sampled repeatedly; episode count is not distinct-world count.

`hp_cost` is the logged **net HP cost**, calculated as HP at task reset after replaying the start prefix minus HP at the current or ending state. Healing can make it negative; changes in maximum HP and survival length also affect it. It is neither accumulated damage taken nor final/remaining HP. The current episode schema does not save starting and final HP separately, so the dashboard cannot reconstruct final HP. Compare this diagnostic within the same start/goal family and outcome, and retain negative values in histograms.

Faults are invalid simulator trajectories, recorded separately from normal episodes. Unknown missing requested records are distinct from explicit unstarted records and observed cutoffs. Duplicate or unexpected observed TaskIDs remain diagnostic rows, are excluded from requested denominators, and invalidate numerical coverage for that rollout. They have no numeric return/floor/HP-cost value unless a value was actually logged; zero must never stand in for missing data. Truncations and collector-budget cuts are unfinished episodes whose numeric values are partial. Numerical distribution filters must show their eligibility and sample count, while outcome denominators retain requested faults, cuts and unstarted tasks. These categories do not establish ordinary defeats. Coverage and missing metric/manifests remain separate from game outcomes.

PPO loss, KL, entropy, clip fraction and throughput are job diagnostics. Episode-family filters cannot reconstruct family-specific optimizer losses from the current summaries. A mean of job means weights jobs equally; a mean over episode samples weights episodes equally. Rolling summaries and histogram views should identify which operation they use. Histogram bins retain signed values, include the rightmost endpoint once, and account for missing/nonfinite values separately. Axis zoom changes the view, not the cohort; explicit range or episode filters change the selected sample.

Whole-game observer comparisons remain the fixed 48 fresh DEV worlds. INITIAL is the immutable pre-update model, SELECTED is the learner's training-stage selected checkpoint, and LATEST is its latest completed candidate. SELECTED is not a model chosen by the observer for whole-game wins. Each observation belongs to the displayed checkpoint SHA and original evaluation time; reusing an unchanged SHA is one measurement, not another independent replicate. A different live SHA is unevaluated until its own panel completes. Repeated DEV wins and mean-floor changes are descriptive monitoring evidence; no held-out final results are exposed.


## V2 continuous condition (default)

Run the service from the **long-curriculum-v2** worktree. The default campaign is `data/long_rl_v2/campaign`; the registered `v1` selector reads the preserved paused campaign in the original long-curriculum worktree. `--campaign` and `--v1-campaign` configure those two destinations at startup. HTTP `campaign=v1|v2` selects a registered source only; arbitrary path values are rejected. Frontend choices are stored separately per condition. Stop/restart only the dashboard service when deploying Python changes; learner lifecycle belongs to the controller.

The new condition starts from the original `57f1e961…` checkpoint and uses the remaining **18,424.127912 active seconds** transferred from the original 10-hour allocation. Its pilot consumes that transfer. The compact budget strip reports original allocation, v1 consumed time and transfer; a fresh allocation is not silently added. The fixed training mix is 70% fresh full starts, 20% Act 2 continuations and 10% Act 3 continuations; all TRAIN tasks target Act 3. The continuing learner and evaluation champion are separate identities. Ordinary DEV regressions do not rewind the learner or reset its optimizer.

V2 consumes controller `journal.evaluations` and `initial_panels`, **not** the v1 observer. The screen (`screen_full3`, 48 requested worlds), confirmation (`confirm_full3`, 192 requested worlds), and Act 1 guard (`retention_act1`, 192 requested worlds) are selectable independently and are never combined into a pooled score. Their new original-checkpoint baselines come from the validated v2 anchor artifacts. The old v1 0/48, floor28.60 is not a v2 baseline and is omitted from the v2 API. Missing baseline/evaluation is NOT YET EVALUATED. Every point retains panel, cohort size, checkpoint SHA, evaluation time and fault/censor context. The champion is selected using the controller's full-game confirmation/retention gates, while screen evidence is routing evidence.

Learning-health cards trace journal-published complete checkpoint ancestry from each head to the original source. They show newly accepted updates retained in learner and champion, committed updates discarded from learner ancestry, cold optimizer starts, warm restores, rollbacks and active elapsed since champion promotion. Missing or broken ancestry stays unavailable. Imported original optimizer steps are not counted as new v2 learning. Alerts are observational: repeated cold starts, a recorded learner rollback, 30 active minutes without a champion promotion, or evaluation exceeding40% of at least10 recent measured completed child runtimes warrant inspection. They never pause, reset, rank or select a learner. Evaluation overhead is a child-runtime diagnostic, not GPU utilization.

V2 logging adds `endpoint_public_v1` per-episode public health: task-reset HP/maxHP after prefix replay; final or censored endpoint HP/maxHP; maximum actually observed act; and bounded start/termination state summaries. Live combat player HP supersedes stale game-context HP. Fields absent in public observations remain null. Faults expose a last-observed summary but have no fabricated final endpoint. Rewards, action sampling, GAE and encoder observations are unchanged; full step trajectories are not logged. `hp_cost` remains net task-reset HP minus ending HP. The explorer offers actual reset/end HP, endpoint HP fraction, and observed-act distributions/scatter; legacy v1 missing health stays unavailable. Absolute ending floor and floors advanced remain separate diagnostics.

V2 adds `panel_id` to `/api/episodes`; the dropdown chooses one independent panel. Registered campaign and file identities enter the cached data context, pagination remains dataset-bound and JSON responses support local gzip. Exact TaskID joins, requested/fault/cutoff/unstarted denominators, numeric eligibility, bounded windows, source filters and CSV provenance are preserved.
