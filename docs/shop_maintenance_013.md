# Shop maintenance and observation migration

The 0.1.3 experimental candidate repairs reached upstream shop defects. On TRAIN world 40000000040, Courier restocks produced 1-gold potion offers; the policy repeatedly purchased them even with full inventory. The old pricing function discarded its base-price argument, discount results were unused, and the action validator permitted ineffective purchases. Those trajectories remain preserved under their original identities.

The tracked native patch now multiplies restock base prices by the existing merchant variance and applies Courier and Membership discounts. It assigns the replacement relic's computed price, removes the duplicate Courier half-discount on replacement cards, applies Courier when acquired in the current shop, and checks the actual acquired egg relic when upgrading shop offers. Potion purchases require space and absence of Sozu; invalid native execution throws before changing gold. No unrelated high-ascension pricing or unsupported character rules are changed.

Package/environment version advances to 0.1.3. The observation label advances to `sts_env_public_v2`: legal shop menus now exclude ineffective potion purchases, so indices can differ. The JSON trace container format remains unchanged, but native/interface identities change. Replay old traces and banks with their original source/build; do not strip their expected provenance. Legacy candidate model tensors can be transferred because the encoder dimensions and field allowlist are unchanged, but their actions and outcomes must be reevaluated on this condition.

The native module is built separately at `external/sts_lightspeed_rl/build013`; the 0.1.2 module remains at `build`. Use `STS_LIGHTSPEED_BUILD` explicitly for standalone commands. No release tag is created or moved. These experiment-branch changes can be ported as a separate clean-environment maintenance change after review.

Five constructed native shop fixtures exercise actual setup through a real map shop, then check restock prices/discounts, full inventory, Sozu, unchanged gold on invalid purchase, card price discounts and new relic charges. They are mechanics regressions, not player performance. Fresh/reverse upstream patch checks and the full suite are recorded in the overnight log. New full-run baselines are required before learning or comparison with the earlier 0.1.2 results.
