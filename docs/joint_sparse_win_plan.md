# Joint sparse-win RL pilot

Branch: `codex/joint-sparse-win-rl`. Scope: Ironclad ascension 0, Acts 1–3, full learned control from fresh starts. Initial actor is round-2 update20 (SHA3d135d9b...). Native0.1.4 binary936734a2... is pinned and reused read-only. Prior branches, snapshots, cohorts and budgets remain untouched. No native search or teacher intervention is used.

## Scientific condition

Reward is exactly1 on a completed Act3 victory,0 on an ordinary defeat and0 on all intermediate decisions. Faults, timeouts, incomplete/censored worlds and unstarted tasks are invalid data, never defeats. Secret Portal final bosses are recognized by public act/room, not fixed floor50. No floor, HP, combat damage or sub-act rewards enter learning. Entropy/rehearsal are explicit optimisation regularizers, not environment reward.

Both combat and OOC actors train from the same complete full-game rollouts. Gamma1/lambda1 Monte-Carlo targets use the final binary outcome at every decision; there is no cross-phase critic bootstrap. Two new independent bounded win-probability critics consume public encodings and train on complete-game labels. Their gradients cannot enter actor encoders. Existing actor checkpoint critic subtrees are frozen and unused for learning. Phase-specific optimisers/loss normalisation prevent combat transition volume from suppressing OOC updates; episode lengths and gradient mass remain visible.

Provisional fixed settings:768 games/iteration, two collectors/six native workers, chunk16; actor LR combat1e-5/OOC3e-5, one epoch, clip.1, minibatch4096/microbatch128, max norm.5, AdamW weight decay0. Sampling uses combat temperature .25/OOC .5, uniform exploration mixture combat .001/OOC .002. Exact mixture log probabilities are stored and reconstructed in PPO; deployment remains exact argmax. Small entropy coefficient.001. Fixed original-policy KL rehearsal coefficient.1 (declared increase to maximum1 when soft drift threshold is exceeded). Critics use LR3e-4. CPU collection and updates. The bounded CPU update screen took 0.53 seconds for 2,048 choice states; the separate device probe exited SIGABRT during its accelerator screen, so CPU was selected. This is an operational choice, not a speed comparison against a completed MPS update.

Initial budget is three active hours including in-run baselines/evaluations. No automatic extension. A run-owned control.json supports graceful stop between stages. Atomic latest/full optimizer/RNG checkpoints, immutable pre-update/evaluated snapshots, exact compressed optimizer buffers, complete action-prefix receipts, adaptive guard state and immutable source/native snapshots preserve recovery. Resume requires matching configuration, source, runtime, native binary, parent and rehearsal identities. In-process transaction rejection restores both optimizer/RNG states. Worker scheduling cannot be recreated from seeds alone; stored optimizer buffers bridge that gap. The learner continues from latest; champion is separate. Stagnation never restores an earlier champion. Only invalid/nonfinite or hard trust/retention failures stop or reject an update, with exact reasons and retained-step accounting.

Fresh TRAIN seeds start60e9; exact single-observation DEV1024 starts61e9. A second DEV confirmation is reserved61.2e9 and opens only for a declared screening candidate. Existing5.8/5.9e9 cohorts and historical FINAL data are not tuned on. Fixed rehearsal is collected before training from 128 fresh greedy TRAIN worlds starting 63e9, stratified across combat/OOC and Acts 1–3, capped at 128 choice states per bucket. Fewer than 16 per bucket blocks launch. Baselines are recorded before updates. Training stochastic win rates and greedy deployment wins are separate curves.

## Failure modes and protections

| Failure | Evidence/concern | Prevention and detection |
|---|---|---|
| Sparse success support | Few wins yield noisy or zero signal | Minimum4 wins and4 defeats before actor updates; bounded larger collection/explicit support failures; no all-zero normalised-advantage updates |
| Long-horizon critic bias | lambda.95 erased useful strategic choices | Gamma/lambda1 terminal outcome targets; phase/act MC calibration; no cross-phase critic bootstraps |
| Critic interference | Shared actor/state encoder can drift under value loss | Independent critics and disjoint parameter-ID tests; actor-only PPO optimisers |
| OOC drowned by combat | Joint transition/minibatch mass imbalance | Separate phase update loops/normalisation/optimisers; phase/act samples, losses, gradients and norms |
| Cumulative forgetting | Small local KL did not preserve original or Act3 wins | Fixed-original rehearsal and phase/act KL/p95/argmax drift; sampled-state drift; full-game/Act1/endgame retention checks |
| Wrong selection objective | Act2 leader rejected full-game winners | Select on full-game wins only; floor/clear rates diagnostic; learner and champion never conflated |
| Repeated rollback | V1 lost98.6% of performed optimisation | Warm continuous learner; transactional update rejection only; retained/discarded step and parent ledger |
| Saturated exploration | OOC alternatives had near-zero probability | Explicit legal uniform mixture and logged effective entropy/top probabilities/action categories; same q in collection/update |
| Invalid reward labels | Faults/cuts or subgoal wins can corrupt terminal labels | Full-run starts/maxAct3; binary reward adapter; whole-world quarantine; exact task/accounted cohort audits |
| Reproducibility/masking | Batch shape, illegal labels and stale likelihoods | Fixed greedy single-row evaluation, pinned runtime; behaviour-ratio-before-update check, legality/nonfinite tests, exact resume proofs |
| Local/generalisation gap | Fits/isolated wins did not transfer | Whole-world holdout diagnostics; paired full-game DEV and original references; fixed-state fit is not a promotion gate |
| Resource waste | Retaining all raw states exhausted memory | Chunked compact encoded buffers/microbatching; RSS/disk/throughput; bounded collection/update and finite native RPCs |
| Dashboard optimism | Reused/missing checkpoints, filtered denominators | Exact checkpoint/cohort IDs; missing stays null; training/eval/source/status filters and requested invalid outcomes shown |
| New risks | Very sparse critic collapse, importance-ratio explosions, successful long games dominating, anchor preventing improvement | Return/advantage distributions, effective sample size, ratios/clipping, critic Brier/calibration, per-world lengths, actor gradient norms and explicit stagnation alerts |

## Guards and metrics

Always log requested/valid/wins/losses/faults/censored/unstarted, Wilson win intervals, task/world coverage, mean/quantile floor and episode length, ending act/public endpoint, final-boss entry/HP, action-category frequencies, phase/act decision counts and stochastic policy entropy/confidence. Record actor and critic losses separately, raw/normalised advantages and MC targets, value means/Brier/explained-variance diagnostics on world-held-out critic labels, behaviour log-prob parity, ratios/ESS/clipping/local KL, original-policy mean/p95 KL and changed argmax, gradient norms, per-update weight L2 displacement and exact weight identity changes, retained/rejected actor/critic steps, throughput/encoding/collection/update/evaluation seconds, learner peak RSS/disk/queue power and active budget.

Hard guards: nonfinite actor updates reject the transaction; illegal menus, mismatched likelihoods and other programming failures stop the run; mean original KL>.05 or bucket p95>.2/argmax drift>.2 rejects and records a transaction; persistent rejection or insufficient sparse support stops rather than silently changing scientific settings. Faults>2% or incomplete collection stop the pilot with preserved evidence. Greedy DEV Act1 net loss>32/1024 or full-win loss>16 with pairedp<.05 stops for review. A small win fluctuation alone is not a reset/promotion. Stagnation after45 active minutes alerts without rewriting the learner. Champion improvement requires paired win gain>=8 andp<=.05 on screen, then a separately saved confirmation before promotion; repeated DEVp values are nominal.

Dashboard will expose wins/retention, head/act health, optimisation, resources, alerts and exact checkpoint lineage, with zoom/pan, metric selectors, phase/act/outcome/dataset filters, episode table and CSV. Read-only localhost service; no live control endpoints or exposure of weights/private fault payloads.

## Bounded preflight evidence (14 October)

The initial sampling recipe (combat T .5, OOC T 1, epsilon .005/.02) won 0/64 fresh games. Its actor change came only from entropy/rehearsal; that smoke proves plumbing, not successful reward learning. The selected conservative recipe won 5/256 on a second namespace (greedy reference 5/128), and 13/256 on the native signal preflight namespace. Cohorts and stochastic streams differ: these small measurements are not a statistically established temperature benefit. They justify testing a recipe with enough observed success support.

The 13-win preflight had zero faults, exact binary episode rewards, maximum reconstructed behavior log-probability error 2.96e-5 and 23 retained actor optimizer steps (17 combat, 6 OOC), with frozen legacy critic hashes unchanged. A separate fresh-process smoke reproduced actor/critic weights and every phase-optimizer tensor exactly. The full regression suite passed 515 tests (three fixture/optional-runtime skips), plus the fixed random native smoke. The 256-world fresh-process recovery/storage check is separately recorded.

Provenance caveat: this candidate package reports `__version__=0.1.4`, while the existing episode diagnostic `environment_version` remains `0.1.3`. The study pins the actual module SHA, Python-interface SHA and archived source; it does not infer identity from the inconsistent string or silently rewrite old artifacts. Correcting that diagnostic belongs to a separate clean-environment maintenance change.

The active-hour clock counts short heartbeat intervals and excludes intervals of at least five seconds (e.g. queue SIGSTOP or system sleep); this is an approximation, recorded in `budget.json`. Limits and operator stop are checked between complete stages, so the last in-flight cohort/update can finish after the nominal deadline. Native RPCs have a 15-second limit, and every collector has its own five-minute no-progress deadline. Keep the laptop available; battery pauses are visible. Dashboard memory is bounded by curated summaries, not training buffers.

Operational recovery: inspect `status.json`, `log.jsonl`, queue state and fault receipts first. To stop gracefully, atomically replace run-owned `control.json` with `{"action":"stop"}`. To resume after a recoverable operational failure, use the identical command/config with `--resume`; do not alter the live source, parameters or original budget. A different scientific condition needs a separate output and ledger. Exact offline optimizer reconstruction loads `before_N.pt`, `buffer_N.pt.gz`, `anchors.pt` and the saved RNG; actor/critic updates use the archived source. Archives and model/data buffers stay local.

The 256-world fresh-process recovery matched both heads, both critics, all phase optimizer tensors and sampler state exactly. Its compressed optimizer buffer is 55,305,480 bytes. Preflight jobs and receipts are under `data/rl_runs/joint_sparse_{preflight,native_signal,probe,cpu,validate}_20261014`. These are diagnostic jobs, separate from the sustained pilot.
