# Results ledger

Timings are exploratory single cohorts without intervals. A repeat of identical work took 15% longer, so timing differences far beyond 15% are called Better or Worse and smaller ones Inconclusive. Teacher-probability verdicts refer only to that preference proxy, with invalid answers scored zero; comparisons from fewer than 10 worlds carry no interval and no verdict. Historical evidence is indexed in [archive](archive.md). Calibrations, warmups, failures and mechanics checks are retained in [the complete CSVs](figures/llm-throughput/serving.csv).
