# Historical research

The pre-CLI LLM work is preserved at the git tag `archive/llm/pre-cli` (commit
a4e7ffa on ws/llm; the tag is local until pushed). Its entry points are
docs/research_plan.md, docs/experiment_history.md,
docs/repository_review_2026-09-05.md and the September Gemma template audits;
read them with `git show archive/llm/pre-cli:docs/<file>`. Those records retain
their original data and interfaces; this new study uses public observations
produced by the released sts_env package instead.

The old phantom-power quarantine was later identified as false positives from
legitimate game effects. Preserve the correction when interpreting its subsets.
