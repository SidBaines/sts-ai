# Round 4: more out-of-combat PPO from the round 3 champion

**Status:** running. **Verdict:** pending.

## Question

Out-of-combat PPO improved every arm of the
[large-batch study](2026-10-20-large-batch-ppo.md), and gained most on the
better combat head (round 3, 315 to 394 of 4,096 fresh worlds). Does a
fourth round, with the hygiene options that helped under the potion filter,
keep improving the new champion, or has it reached the limit of what PPO
can find at its current entropy? Separately: does the Demon Form rule,
which helped every earlier player, still add anything on top of round 3,
which beats the rule's stack without it? (Approach:
[out-of-combat PPO](../strategies.md).)

## Design

- **Start:** the champion of 21 October (round 3, `champion_7d876c939a74.pt`),
  combat head frozen and played greedily.
- **Round 4** (`round4_20261021/round4`): arm 4's settings from the
  large-batch study: 24 iterations of 3,072 games, lambda 1, weight decay
  0, learning rate 0.0003, 3 epochs, minibatch 4,096, clip 0.2, KL guard
  0.05, potion filter, detached critic, normalised value targets, entropy
  bonus 0.01 on strategic menus; training seed 1, so the training worlds
  are new (every earlier arm used seed 0).
- **Demon Form test:** the champion with the Demon Form rule as a menu
  overlay (take Demon Form whenever offered), paired with the champion.
- **Evaluation:** `fast_eval.py` under the study protocol (8 processes of
  32 games) on the 4,096 second-DEV worlds, paired with the champion's 413
  wins, and on 4,096 unused worlds from 95,000,000,000, paired with the
  champion's own games there. Round 4's best checkpoint by the trainer's
  evaluation is also run on second DEV.
- **Decision rules, fixed in advance:** round 4's final checkpoint, or the
  Demon Form overlay, is *Better* if it wins more than the champion on
  second DEV with exact McNemar p < 0.05, and is confirmed if it also wins
  more on the worlds from 95,000,000,000. Round 4's best-by-evaluation
  checkpoint is exploratory. A confirmed Demon Form gain is distilled into
  the policy before any further PPO.
- **Monitoring:** the trainer's evaluation every 4 iterations on 1,024 DEV
  worlds from 91,000,000,000 (study protocol), entropy, KL and half-batch
  gradient cosines.
- **Budget:** about 2.5 hours of training and four 4,096-world evaluations
  on the laptop.
- **Approval:** Sid, 21 October ("Okay sounds good, please continue"),
  after the proposal to promote round 3, test the Demon Form rule on top of
  it and run round 4 with the hygiene options.
