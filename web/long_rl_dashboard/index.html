Measurement and data boundaries

Read-only localhost telemetry. Active time excludes pauses and offline gaps; wall time includes them. Histogram and scatter numeric eligibility excludes faults and cutoffs by default while outcome counts retain every loaded requested record. HP cost is net change from task reset after any saved prefix, not total damage or final HP. Floors are absolute ending floors, not floors advanced. New final worlds remain embargoed; reused DEV evidence does not establish significance or mastery. Missing gradient norm is not inferred from configured clipping. No checkpoint model bytes or raw action prefixes are loaded. Unexpected/duplicate diagnostic records never enter requested denominators or numeric plots. Invalid coverage is excluded from numeric analysis. Selecting an outcome conditions the denominator on that outcome.
